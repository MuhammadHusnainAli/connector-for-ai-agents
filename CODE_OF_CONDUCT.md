# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about code, not about the person
writing it.

## What we expect

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason and, where you can, a suggestion.
- Accept feedback the same way. Reviewers are trying to make the change better,
  not to judge you.
- Respect that people contribute in their own time, from different countries,
  time zones, and levels of experience. A slow reply is not a slight.
- Ask questions. "I don't understand why this works" is a useful comment.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory remarks — including about someone's
  identity, background, or level of experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to
  relitigate them, or badgering maintainers for a response.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and the project's advisory channels — and whenever someone is
representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **muhammad.husnain.ali.738@gmail.com**. Reports are read by
the maintainer and kept confidential; you will get a reply within 7 days.

Tell us what happened, where, and — if you can — link to it. You do not have to
prove your case to be taken seriously, and you will not be asked to confront the
person involved.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will act in proportion to what happened, which may mean a private
word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a permanent ban from the
project. Anyone enforcing this document is bound by it too; failing to enforce it
fairly is itself a problem worth reporting.
